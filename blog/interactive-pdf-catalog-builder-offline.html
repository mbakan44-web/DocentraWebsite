Interactive Catalog Builder: Turn Static PDFs into Clickable Product Catalogs

📅 November 15, 2026 • ⏱️ 5 min read • 👤 Docentra Product Team

Customers browsing a 40-page product brochure rarely want to copy-paste SKU codes into a search bar. With Docentra Interactive Catalog Builder, you can drag clickable product hotspots directly onto any PDF page, attach product titles, descriptions, price tags, and direct Web Store or WhatsApp order links, and export a single standalone .html file—without paying recurring SaaS fees.

Why Businesses Love Standalone Interactive Catalogs

  • Visual Hotspot Editor: Click directly on any product photo inside your PDF to place a pulsing hotspot pin or bounding box.
  • Rich Product Cards & Direct Ordering: When a buyer clicks a hotspot, a sleek modal displays the product name, SKU, price, description, and an instant Buy Online / Order via WhatsApp button.
  • Self-Contained HTML5 Output: Export a single offline-ready .html catalog that works in any desktop or mobile browser—ideal for email campaigns, trade shows, and dealer portals.
  • 10-Language Interface: Build and export catalogs in 10 languages including English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Arabic, Japanese, and Chinese.
