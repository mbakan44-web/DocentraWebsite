Sending clients or investors five separate PDF attachments—annual report, product catalog, price list, technical datasheet, and company profile—creates friction. Docentra Digital Document Portfolio unifies multiple PDFs into a single, branded collection with category tags, live preview, and dual export options.

Two Professional Ways to Deliver Your Portfolio

  • Standalone Interactive HTML5 Portfolio (.html): Generates a sleek web-style portal with your brand name, accent color, category filters, and embedded PDF pages—playable offline in any browser.

  • Live Preview & 10-Language UI: Reorder documents, customize titles and categories, preview the final portal instantly, and export in any of Docentra's 10 supported languages.
