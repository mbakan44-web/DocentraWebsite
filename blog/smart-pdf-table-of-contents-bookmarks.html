Smart Table of Contents: Auto-Generate Clickable PDF TOC & Bookmarks Offline

📅 November 15, 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read • 👤 Docentra Product Team

Long technical manuals, tenders, and academic theses often arrive as 300-page PDFs with zero bookmarks or navigation structure. Docentra Smart Table of Contents lives right in the left sidebar next to Thumbnails—letting you auto-scan headings, jump to any section in one click, and permanently embed both PDF Bookmarks and a clickable Table of Contents page.

How Smart TOC Works

  • Left-Sidebar Contents Tab: Switch between Thumbnails and Contents directly in the PDF viewer; existing bookmarks load automatically, or click Auto Scan to detect numbered headings (1., 1.1., 2.3), chapters, and uppercase titles.
  • Full Hierarchy Editor: Rename titles, adjust indentation levels (1–3), reorder rows, or add custom sections.
  • Dual PDF Output: Write native hierarchical PDF Outlines (Bookmarks) and optionally insert a clean, clickable Table of Contents page at the front of the document with automatic page-number shifting.

Navigate 300-Page PDFs Effortlessly

Open any long PDF in Docentra v1.0.7 and switch to the left Contents tab to scan headings instantly.
