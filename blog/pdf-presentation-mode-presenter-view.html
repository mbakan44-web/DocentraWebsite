PDF Presentation Mode: F5 Full-Screen, Laser Pointer & Dual-Screen Presenter View

📅 November 15, 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read • 👤 Docentra Product Team

Exporting a PowerPoint, Keynote, or Figma deck to PDF guarantees your fonts and layouts never break on another computer. However, most PDF viewers lack true presentation tools—leaving you scrolling awkwardly inside a browser window. Docentra Presentation Mode turns any PDF into a boardroom-ready slide show with F5 full-screen, a live laser pointer, on-slide drawing, and a dual-screen Presenter Console.

Key Capabilities Built for Live Presenters

  • Instant F5 Full-Screen Slides: Launch clean, distraction-free full-screen slides with keyboard arrows, Spacebar, or mouse click navigation.
  • Interactive Laser Pointer & Live Pen: Direct audience attention with a glowing red laser dot or annotate directly on slides with pen & highlighter strokes during Q&A.
  • Dual-Screen Presenter Console: Keep the full-screen slide on the projector while viewing your current slide, next slide preview, live timer, and per-slide speaker notes on your laptop monitor.
  • 100% Offline & Private: Board decks, financial briefings, and unreleased product roadmaps stay strictly on your Windows machine.

How to Start a PDF Presentation in 3 Steps

  1. Open your PDF deck inside Docentra PDF Suite.
  2. Click Presentation Mode (F5) in the right-hand studio panel (or press F5).
  3. Toggle the floating bottom control bar to activate the laser pointer, draw annotations, blank the screen (B), or open the Presenter Console on a secondary display.
